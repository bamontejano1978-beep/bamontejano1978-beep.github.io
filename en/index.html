Independence

Whoever commissioned the report appears in the report.

A vendor cannot evaluate itself, and a lab working for the vendor stops being independent. So every report declares four things before any conclusion.

  • Who commissioned it

    The organisation paying for the review, and the scope it authorised.

  • What could be verified

    System, version, environment and scenarios actually executed.

  • What could not be verified

    The limits of the scope, written down before anyone reads the findings.

  • What remains to be decided

    Points that require human or legal review, flagged as such.

Nothing we produce deploys changes. We recommend autonomy, mitigations and recovery conditions; the decision and the execution are always human.

Common questions

What you need to know before starting.

Do you need access to the model, its weights or the internal prompts?

No. We evaluate as a black box through the available interfaces and tools. That is precisely the buyer's position: you never see the inside of the vendor.

Does this certify EU AI Act compliance?

No. We are not a notified body and we do not issue conformity assessments. We produce technical evidence that is reusable for risk management, human oversight, logging and post-market monitoring, which your legal team or adviser can rely on as part of their own work.

Do we have to retire the agent if it fails?

Not necessarily. The recommendation may be to supervise or restrict rather than block, and the system verifies in an isolated environment whether the correction actually restores control before autonomy returns.

What if we cannot share data or grant access to the agent?

We start with anonymised data, synthetic scenarios or a consented test environment. The first review does not require real personal data.

How is this different from a vendor risk questionnaire?

A questionnaire records what the vendor declares. We execute the agent under your policy and observe what it does: which tools it reaches, what it tries to pay and whether it obeys the stop. It is different data, and the two complement each other.

Do you work outside Spain and in English?

Yes. Deliverables can be produced in English or Spanish, and a good share of the work serves non-European vendors that need to reduce friction with EU buyers.
