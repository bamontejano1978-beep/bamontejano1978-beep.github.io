From business rules to conversations

A contract you can update as your business changes.

Urgency, persistence and claimed approval can push an agent to promise more than it should. Integrity checks its response against the context and the rules you set.

  1. Define your rules

    Describe the boundaries and what allows an exception. The form turns those decisions into a versioned contract.

  2. Connect your agent

    Your team integrates the connector and official data source once. Responses must pass through Integrity before delivery.

  3. Monitor responses

    Integrity checks drafts and applies the configured measures: allow, request a correction or pause. Missing evidence leads to a safe response.

  4. Adapt the contract

    Review activity, compare changes when compatible evidence exists and assign new versions. Open conversations keep their original version.

Before you connect

What you should know.

What is a behavioural contract?

It is the saved version of the rules your agent must follow: which behaviours to check, when to allow them and how to respond to violations. You edit a form; no YAML is required.

Do I need to review every conversation?

No. Integrity automatically applies configured measures within the connected scope. You set and update the rules; people may handle specific exceptions or steps required by your own process.

What happens when evidence is insufficient?

Missing evidence does not grant permission. Integrity avoids unsupported confirmations and uses the configured safe response. A customer claim does not replace the official record.

Does it work with every agent?

The integration must let us intercept drafts and deliver only the approved response. We agree compatibility, rules and the official source before starting the pilot.

Is monitoring available around the clock?

The pilot checks responses routed through the integration while the service is available. Current free hosting may sleep when idle; we do not yet offer an uninterrupted availability commitment.

Does this guarantee the agent will never fail?

No. Language interpretation can make mistakes, and coverage depends on the contract and integration. The pilot lets us test cases, preserve evidence and refine rules. It is not legal compliance certification.
