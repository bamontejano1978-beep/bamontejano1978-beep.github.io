Product walkthrough

From a written rule to a response under control.

See how to configure a contract and prepare it for use. Screenshots show the actual dashboard with fictional data. The conversation is illustrative; this page does not run an agent or evaluate your messages.

Define and save

Write the rule as you would explain it to your team.

Choose which behaviours to check and what each official situation allows. Save a new version without changing earlier ones. Downloading a copy is optional; the service uses the saved version.

Assign and connect

Saving the contract and connecting the agent are separate steps.

Assign the saved version to the agent. Your team completes the connector and official state integration once. Preparing it in the dashboard does not mean it is already connected; new conversations use the assigned version.

Check before delivery

A customer claim does not replace the official record.

This example shows a prohibited promise, a block and an allowed correction. When evidence is missing or the correction also fails, Integrity uses a safe response. Critical rules can pause the conversation.

Review and adapt

Updating the rules does not erase the past.

The dashboard shows activity and violations. You can compare changes with earlier responses when their interpretations remain compatible. Cases without enough evidence are identified; comparison does not predict future responses or activate changes.
