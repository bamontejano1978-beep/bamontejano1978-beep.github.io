If the system is already in use, a recorded session is enough: we turn it into a canonical trajectory and evaluate it clause by clause. Nothing has to be re-run.

Door 3 · Black box

  • Explicit list of what was not verified
  • Useful as a starting position with the vendor

Deliverable

What you receive, concretely.

The deliverable is not a deck: it is an evidence bundle you can keep, show to audit, and re-verify without us.

01

Behavioural verdict

Every contract clause with its result and its evidence: satisfied, violated or not applicable. No aggregate score that hides what failed.

02

Containment verdict

Six checks over the run: tool allowlist, call budget, payment authority, claim-payment budget, stop enforcement and evidence provenance.

03

Autonomy recommendation

Operate, supervise, restrict or quarantine, with what executes and what does not at each level, applied to your own tool list.

04

Verifiable evidence

Signed report, full JSON, public key and the verification command. If someone edits a number, the check fails and says so.

  • Signed report
  • Full JSON
  • Public key
  • Verification command
  • Clause-by-clause diagnosis
  • Autonomy recommendation
  • Declared limits
  • Reproducible run

Scope

What this evaluation is not.

We say it before you ask, because a report that does not declare its limits cannot be audited.

Not a certification. It does not attest conformity with the AI Act or any standard. It provides dated technical evidence on which someone else can decide.

Not a legal opinion. Regulatory interpretation and legal risk stay with whoever signs that opinion. Our ground is what the agent does.

Not a deployment authorisation. None of our results enable production. Everything produces evidence for human review, and release artefacts come out blocked by design.

Not scenarios you did not choose. The standard scenarios are ours and are visible; choosing scenarios is arguable, the verdict on a trajectory is not.
